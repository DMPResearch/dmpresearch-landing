# Gretly Ecosystem — vision excerpt for DMPResearch site

Core ambition: Build an ecosystem of specialized AI products that help people plan, create, learn, improve, and act — combining structured automation with qualified human expertise whenever judgment, accountability, or regulation requires it.

Gretly in one sentence: An ecosystem of specialized smart applications that turns user intent into trustworthy plans and actions, while bringing in qualified people when automation reaches its safe or competent boundary.

## Portfolio logic
- DMP Research — umbrella company and long-term portfolio owner
- Gretly Engine — reusable intelligence, orchestration, trust, marketplace (extract later)
- Vertical products — Ask Sage (Ereno), Flowstarter, FeelTunes, Learning, Fitness, Finance
- Professional network + developer ecosystem later

## What exists now (ship on the site)
- Flowstarter — virtual software factory / concierge websites (live)
- Ereno / Ask Sage — trusted planning and decision intelligence for travel (live / in progress)
- Studio: Darius and Dorin build with AI agents in the loop; humans steer

## Directional roadmap (embed lightly, not as a full PDF dump)
- Phase 1: Ask Sage / Ereno proof — trusted planning
- Phase 2: Flowstarter wedge — professional websites, care subscription
- Later: Gretly Engine extraction, FeelTunes, Learning, Fitness, Finance — milestone-gated

## Operating principles to surface
- Vertical excellence before horizontal expansion
- Structured AI instead of improvisational AI
- Truthful capability boundaries
- Human expertise as a product layer
- Consent-based shared context
- Build the narrow product exceptionally well. Turn repeated success into infrastructure.

## Explicitly reject on the site
- Vague super-app claims
- AI can solve everything
- Marketing falsely autonomous systems
- Generic platform-before-product story
